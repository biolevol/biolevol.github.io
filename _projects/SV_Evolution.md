I am developing a simulation framework to investigate how recurrent mutation, recombination, and selection shape the evolution of structurally complex loci. The framework follows structural haplotypes and the rearrangements connecting them, rather than treating copy number as the only evolving feature.

The amylase locus provides a starting point for asking how existing sequence architecture changes the rearrangements that can occur next, and how recurrent mutation affects the interpretation of population differences.
