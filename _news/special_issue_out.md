The special issue Functional and Adaptive Effects of Genomic Structural Variation has now been published in Heredity. I am grateful to all contributors for their work and to my co-editors Ellen, Megan, and Omer for their collaboration.

The full collection is available [here](https://www.nature.com/collections/deejeiccee)
