I visited **Leo Speidel’s lab at iTHEMS, RIKEN**, in Japan during October–November 2025.

During the visit, I presented *Why complexity persists: Evolutionary dynamics of the amylase locus in primates* at the iTHEMS Biology Seminar. The visit supported our ongoing collaboration on archaic introgression and structural variation.
