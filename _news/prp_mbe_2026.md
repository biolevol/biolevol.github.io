Our paper **Multiscale structural evolution of proline-rich protein genes shapes primate salivary defenses** is now published in *Molecular Biology and Evolution*.

The study combines long-read assemblies and comparative genomics to investigate the structural evolution of salivary proline-rich protein genes across primates.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msag224).
