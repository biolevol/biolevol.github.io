In March, I visited the Flatt Lab and Malaspinas Lab in Switzerland and gave two talks:

- **Flatt Lab, University of Fribourg:** _Origins of recurrent structural variation: lessons from the primate amylase locus_.
- **Malaspinas Lab, University of Lausanne:** _Why complexity persists: Evolutionary dynamics of the amylase locus in humans and non-human primates_.

Thank you to both labs for hosting me and for the discussions about structural variation and genome evolution.
