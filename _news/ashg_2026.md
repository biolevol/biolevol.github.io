At **ASHG 2026 in Montréal**, I presented our poster *Integrating long-read assemblies and ancestry graphs to map Neanderthal-introgressed structural variation in humans*. The abstract received **Reviewers’ Choice Abstract** recognition.

I also moderated the platform abstract session *Move Over SNPs: Structural Variants, Repeat Expansions, and More*.

Our ongoing research combines long-read assemblies with ancestry graphs to investigate the contribution of archaic introgression to structural variation in present-day humans.
