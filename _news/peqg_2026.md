I attended the **Population, Evolutionary, and Quantitative Genetics Conference (PEQG)** at Asilomar in Pacific Grove, California, where I presented *Integrating long-read assemblies and ancestry graphs to map Neanderthal-introgressed structural variation in humans* and served as a poster judge.

It was also a pleasure to celebrate **Carsyn Bonesteel’s GSA poster award** for her undergraduate research.
