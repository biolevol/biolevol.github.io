Our **Talk Nerdy to Me** event brought approximately **30 people** together at Fattey Beer Co. in Amherst for an evening of science and conversation.

Organized by the **UB postdoctoral community**, the event gives researchers a chance to share their work with general audiences through short talks and informal discussion. Shikha Jain, Carolyn Graham, Kasturika Shankar, and I presented at the event, with plenty of questions from the audience.
