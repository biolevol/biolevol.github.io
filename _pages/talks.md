## Conference contributions

### 2026

- **ASHG, Montréal, Canada.** Poster: *Integrating long-read assemblies and ancestry graphs to map Neanderthal-introgressed structural variation in humans*. **Reviewers’ Choice Abstract.** I also moderated the platform session *Move Over SNPs: Structural Variants, Repeat Expansions, and More*.
- **Population, Evolutionary, and Quantitative Genetics (PEQG), Pacific Grove, California, USA.** Poster: *Integrating long-read assemblies and ancestry graphs to map Neanderthal-introgressed structural variation in humans*. I also served as a poster judge.
- **Virtual Evolution.** Moderator, Evolutionary Theory session.

### 2025

- **ESEB, Barcelona, Spain.** Talk: *Convergent evolution through recurrent rearrangements and regulatory rewiring in the primate amylase locus*. Poster: *Reconstruction of the human amylase locus reveals ancient duplications seeding modern-day variation*.
- **Ecological and Evolutionary Genomics Gordon Research Conference, Il Ciocco, Italy.** Talk: *Why this locus? Recurrent duplications and extensive structural polymorphism at the amylase locus in humans and non-human primates*. I also chaired the associated Gordon Research Seminar with Joaquin Nunez and co-chaired the GRC Power Hour.
- **Evolution, Athens, Georgia, USA.** Talk: *Convergent evolution through recurrent rearrangements in the primate amylase locus*. Poster: *Reconstruction of the human amylase locus reveals ancient duplications seeding modern-day variation*.
- **Virtual Evolution.** Talks on the human amylase locus and convergent evolution through recurrent rearrangements in the primate amylase locus. Moderator, Population Genetics Theory and Molecular Evolution sessions.
