## Outreach and public engagement

### Talk Nerdy to Me

I established **Talk Nerdy to Me**, a public science event organized by the **UB postdoctoral community**, to give researchers opportunities to share their work with general audiences and practice public speaking. The series brings science into an informal setting where people can meet researchers and ask questions about their work.

### Breaking Bread

In August 2025, Thomas Hitchcock and I organized **Breaking Bread: Exploring the Evolution of Amylase in Humans** at Barcelona’s Festa Major de Gràcia, as part of ESEB’s outreach initiative. Our bilingual English/Spanish activities introduced children and families to human evolution through food, play and interactive demonstrations focused on amylase.

[Read about the event and see photographs]({{ '/news/Gracia_ESEB_outreach/' | relative_url }}).

## Community building

Since 2022, I have organized the **Genome Structure, Evolution and Anthropology Seminar Series** at the University at Buffalo, bringing together researchers working on genome evolution, structural variation and anthropology. The [news archive]({{ '/news/' | relative_url }}) includes seminar schedules.

In 2024, I initiated **Postdoctoral Meetups** at UB to encourage connection and mutual support among postdocs across disciplines.

## Editorial and reviewing service

I have served as an **Associate Editor for Heredity** since September 1, 2025. I also co-edited the special issue [Functional and Adaptive Effects of Genomic Structural Variation](https://www.nature.com/collections/deejeiccee) with Omer Gokcumen, Megan Dennis and Ellen Leffler. The collection and our accompanying editorial were published in 2026.

My professional training includes the GSA Journals Peer Review Training Program and the eLife Early-Career Reviewers Pool, both in 2025. I served on UB’s Distinguished Postdoc Mentor Award selection committee in 2025–2026 and as a poster grader at the Biological Sciences Research Symposium in 2025 and 2026.
