Outreach and public engagement

Talk Nerdy to Me

I established Talk Nerdy to Me, a public science event organized by the UB postdoctoral community, to give researchers opportunities to share their work with general audiences and practice public speaking.

The inaugural event took place at Fattey Beer Co. in Amherst on June 18, 2026, bringing together approximately 30 attendees. Short talks by Shikha Jain, Carolyn Graham, Kasturika Shankar, and myself were followed by audience questions and discussion. The series brings science into an informal setting where people can meet researchers and ask questions about their work.

Breaking Bread

In August 2025, Thomas Hitchcock and I organized Breaking Bread: Exploring the Evolution of Amylase in Humans at Barcelona’s Festa Major de Gràcia, as part of ESEB’s outreach initiative. Our bilingual English/Spanish activities introduced children and families to human evolution through food, play, and interactive demonstrations focused on amylase.

Read about the event and see photographs.

Community building

Since 2022, I have organized the Genome Structure, Evolution and Anthropology Seminar Series at the University at Buffalo, bringing together researchers working on genome evolution, structural variation, and anthropology. The news archive includes seminar schedules.

In 2024, I initiated Postdoctoral Meetups at UB to encourage connection and mutual support among postdocs across disciplines.

Editorial and reviewing service

I have served as an Associate Editor for Heredity since September 1, 2025. I also co-edited the special issue Functional and Adaptive Effects of Genomic Structural Variation with Omer Gokcumen, Megan Dennis, and Ellen Leffler. The collection and our accompanying editorial were published in 2026.

I review manuscripts for Cell, Cell Genomics, Communications Biology, Genetics, Genome Research, Molecular Biology and Evolution, NAR Genomics and Bioinformatics, Nature, Nature Communications, Science Advances, and The American Journal of Human Genetics. I have reviewed grant applications for the Leakey Foundation and NordForsk.

My professional training includes the GSA Journals Peer Review Training Program and the eLife Early-Career Reviewers Pool, both in 2025. I served on UB’s Distinguished Postdoc Mentor Award selection committee in 2025–2026 and as a poster grader at the Biological Sciences Research Symposium in 2025 and 2026.
